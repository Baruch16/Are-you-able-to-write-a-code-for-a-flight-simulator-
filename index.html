Controls

Flying

↓/S pull (nose up) · ↑/W push (nose down)

←→ / AD roll · ZX rudder / nose-wheel steering

Ctrl + stick keys = fine (small) inputs

PgUp/PgDn or +/− throttle · Home full · End idle

[ ] pitch trim · T trim to current stick

M mouse-flying mode (mouse = stick)

Aircraft

G gear · F flaps down · Shift+F flaps up · B airbrake

Space wheel brakes · , . left/right brake · P parking brake

L landing light · N next NAV destination · Q flares

Ctrl+E automatic engine start

Looking around (your head)

Right-drag (or left-drag on the sky) to look anywhere — up, down to the panel, over your shoulder

Shift + arrows: look · Wheel: zoom (or turn a knob under the mouse) · Middle click/C: centre

1 forward · 2 panel · 3 left console · 4 right console

Cockpit

Simulator

Esc pause/menu · I instructor box · H HUD · K sim speed (1×, 2×, 4×, ½×) · Ctrl+R restart

Joystick / gamepad

Flight stick: X/Y = roll/pitch, throttle axis, twist = rudder, trigger = brakes.

Gamepad: left stick = stick, triggers = throttle, bumpers = rudder, right stick = look, A brake, B gear, X/Y flaps, D-pad trim/airbrake.
